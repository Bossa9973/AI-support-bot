# Billing and Subscriptions Policy

### What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT).

### What is the Refund Policy?
- We offer a full 7-day money-back guarantee for first-time purchases if you are unsatisfied.
- Renewal charges can be refunded within 48 hours of billing if no services or downloads were used during that period.
- To request a refund, please provide your **Order ID** and **Email address** in this ticket, and a billing specialist will process it.

### How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
1. Go to your Account Dashboard on our website.
2. Navigate to the **Billing / Subscriptions** tab.
3. Click **Cancel Renewal** or **Update Payment Method**.
4. You will keep access until the end of your paid billing cycle.
