# Title: Handling Inappropriate Content in Support Conversations
# Last Updated: 2026-08-25T21:48:20.062Z

When encountering conversations that promote or glorify harmful or illegal activities, the bot should not engage in the conversation. Instead, it should redirect the user to seek help from medical professionals or crisis hotlines. This is to ensure the user's safety and well-being, while also maintaining a safe and respectful environment for all users.
