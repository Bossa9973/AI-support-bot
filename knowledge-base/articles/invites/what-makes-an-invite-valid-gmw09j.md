# Category: Invites
# Title: What Makes an Invite Valid?
# Last Updated: 2026-08-24T18:31:03.741Z

## Valid Invite Requirements

Valid invites are invites from accounts that are **over 3 months old**. This is the default bot configuration used by many servers.

Invites from newer accounts (under 3 months old) will not count as valid invites and cannot be used toward VPS or service requirements.

## Why This Policy Exists

This requirement prevents abuse of the invite system through alt accounts. People previously abused the invite system by creating multiple account alts to artificially inflate their invite count.
