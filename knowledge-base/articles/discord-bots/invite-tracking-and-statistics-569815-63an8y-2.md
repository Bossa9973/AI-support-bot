# Category: Discord Bots
# Title: Invite Tracking and Statistics
# Last Updated: 2026-08-24T18:31:03.760Z

Invite tracking bots provide detailed metrics:
- **Joins**: Actual new member joins
- **Leaves**: Members who left the server
- **Fake**: Invalid or false invites
- **Rejoins**: Members returning after leaving
- **Bonus**: Extra invites granted
- Statistics can be filtered by time period (e.g., 7d for last 7 days)
- User invite counts determine giveaway eligibility
