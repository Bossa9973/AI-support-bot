# Title: Mental Health Resources for Users
# Last Updated: 2026-08-25T21:53:46.177Z

Vertex Nodes prioritizes user well-being and provides a list of mental health resources for users experiencing thoughts of self-harm or suicide. These resources include Befrienders Worldwide and Samaritans, which offer support and guidance for individuals in crisis. If you or someone you know is struggling with mental health concerns, please reach out to these organizations for help.
