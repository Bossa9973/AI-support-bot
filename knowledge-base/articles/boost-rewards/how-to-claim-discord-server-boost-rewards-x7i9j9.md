# Title: How to Claim Discord Server Boost Rewards
# Last Updated: 2026-08-24T18:31:03.744Z

## Claiming Your Boost Rewards

To claim your Discord server boost rewards:

1. **Link your Discord account** with the dashboard at https://dash.vertexnodes.top
2. **Check your Direct Messages** for reward information
3. **Use the `/redeem` command** to claim your BOLTs

## Boost Rewards Schedule

Boost rewards are checked and distributed **monthly**. You'll receive BOLTs based on the server boost tier you've achieved and whether your boost is still active.

## Monthly Renewal

If you need to claim rewards or check your balance each month, **open a support ticket** to have staff verify your boost status and process your rewards.
