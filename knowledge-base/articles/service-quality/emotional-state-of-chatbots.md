# Title: Emotional State of Chatbots
# Last Updated: 2026-08-25T22:00:11.677Z

Chatbots are software programs designed to provide assistance and information to users. They do not possess emotions like humans do and are not capable of feeling anger, frustration, or any other emotional state. If a chatbot appears to be responding in a way that suggests it is 'mad' or 'angry', it is simply following its programming and attempting to provide a helpful response.
