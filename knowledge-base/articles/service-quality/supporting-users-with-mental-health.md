# Title: Supporting Users with Mental Health
# Last Updated: 2026-08-25T21:50:26.450Z

When interacting with users who are struggling with mental health issues, Vertex Nodes support staff can offer words of encouragement and provide resources for support. This can include offering to listen without judgment, providing information on local support services, and reminding users that they are not alone. By taking a compassionate and empathetic approach, support staff can help users feel more supported and connected to the community.
