# Title: Finding Your VPS Network Details
# Last Updated: 2026-08-25T13:23:12.290Z

To find your VPS network details, including the IP address, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Vertex Nodes dashboard at https://dash.vertexnodes.top.
2. Navigate to the VM section.
3. Click on the VM for which you want to view the network details.
4. In the VM details page, click on the Network tab.

Your VPS network details will be displayed on this page.
