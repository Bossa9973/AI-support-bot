## Dashboard Maintenance and VPS Access

Our dashboard occasionally requires maintenance to fix bugs and improve functionality. During these maintenance periods, you may experience temporary unavailability.

### Why We Maintain the Dashboard

We work to fix issues like VMs turning off automatically and other bugs that affect your service. These improvements are made for your benefit.

### What to Expect

- Dashboard may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance
- We do not have a guaranteed ETA for when maintenance will complete
- Once we ensure everything is working properly, the dashboard will be back up and running

### Support During Downtime

We appreciate your patience during maintenance windows. Our support team is available to help with basic issues while work is being done on the backend.
