# General Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the support hours?
Our automated AI support is available 24/7. Our human moderation and support team reviews tickets Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST.

### How do I open and close tickets?
- To open a ticket: Click the **"📩 Create Ticket"** button on the support panel.
- To close a ticket: Click the **"🔒 Close"** button inside the ticket channel, or type `/close`. Once closed, an HTML transcript will be automatically saved and sent to you via DM.

### Can I invite another user or teammate to my ticket?
Yes! You or staff can type `/add @User` to grant them access to this private ticket channel. To remove someone, type `/remove @User`.

### How can I talk to a human support agent?
If your question cannot be solved by the AI assistant or requires account-level permissions, simply say *"I'd like to speak with staff"* or click the **"Claim"** button. A staff member will take over the ticket.
